Who We Are

The Fraternal Order of the Seven Seas (FOSS) is a fraternity anchored in brotherhood, integrity, and service to humanity.


Mission: To foster a brotherhood of principled leaders who lead through service, uplift communities, and sail as one.


Vision: A united fleet of compassionate leaders making kindness visible in every shore we touch.

Anchored in Values

⚓ Compassion

Serving with heart

🤝 Humility

Leading by serving

🧭 Integrity

True north in all we do

🌊 Brotherhood

One Fleet, One Family

Community Outreach

"Be the reason someone believes that goodness still exists in this world."

We believe that kindness, no matter how small, can create meaningful change. Through reaching out to our communities, supporting those in need, and extending a helping hand, may we keep serving with compassion, humility, and integrity.
