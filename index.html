About

Founded by mariners and maritime enthusiasts, FOSS is a global brotherhood committed to fellowship, tradition, and service on and off the sea. We uphold honor, duty, and community across the seven seas.

Values

Honor · Fellowship · Service · Stewardship · Integrity. Guiding principles that anchor our members to act with respect for the ocean, one another, and the communities we support.

Community Outreach

We volunteer with coastal communities, supporting elder care, clean water initiatives, and maritime safety education programs worldwide.
